# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

SM+ Toolbox is a small hobby project, and the community around it should feel just as approachable as the app itself. Everyone who takes part in this project, in any role, deserves to be treated with respect. We pledge to keep this space friendly, inclusive, and constructive for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Expected behavior

To keep the community a pleasant place, we ask everyone to:

- **Be kind and respectful.** Disagreeing with an idea is fine; attacking a person is not.
- **Be constructive.** When pointing out a problem, offer context or a suggestion if you can.
- **Be patient and welcoming.** Not everyone here is an experienced developer. Questions from beginners are opportunities, not annoyances.
- **Assume good faith.** Most misunderstandings come from short messages, not bad intent. Ask for clarification before reacting.
- **Stay on topic.** Keep issues and pull requests focused on the project so they stay useful for everyone.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behaviors are not welcome in any project space:

- Harassment, personal attacks, or insults, in public or in private.
- Discriminatory jokes or language, including anything sexist, racist, or otherwise demeaning.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or derailing discussions.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as a physical address, without their permission.
- Any other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, code review comments, commit messages, the wiki, and any other space that represents the project. It also applies when you represent the project in other communities, for example as a maintainer at an external event.

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the maintainer, [AldySan](https://github.com/AldySan), directly through GitHub. Include links and context where you can: they help a lot. All reports are reviewed and taken seriously.

Consequences are at the discretion of the maintainer and depend on the severity of the behavior. They range from a request to change behavior, to a temporary ban, to a permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Maintainers who do not follow this code of conduct in good faith may face the same consequences as anyone else.
