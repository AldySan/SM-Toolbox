## Additional gaps found and fixed while verifying

- Terminal dock status colors in light theme (R-25): the terminal surface is dark in both themes, but its chips and notes used the theme's ok/warn/err, so light-theme chips sat near 2-3:1. The `.terminal` scope now pins the dark-theme status and faint values, and the test asserts they stay in sync (#41).
- Update badge, ready-state home title and brand mark in light theme (R-25): sub-AA pairs on soft tints the original audit had not enumerated. Solved by the deeper light accents of the own palette (#40); worst pair 4.63:1.
- Main-process user-facing strings with em dashes (R-02): verifier notes, tool and payload errors, the appearance step preview; rendered in the same UI as renderer copy (#42).
